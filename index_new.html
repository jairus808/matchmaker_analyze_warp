Thesis Abstract

CPSC 4900 · Jairus Rhoades

analyze_warp.py is used to examine the real-time alignment output of the previously implemented Matchmaker score-following library and to expose internal per-frame metrics in an approach for visualization through a TouchDesigner project. The script extracts per-frame metrics including changes in score and performance indices, estimated tempo ratios, and event markers for clamping and score jumps. These outputs are examined across different sampling and hop-length configurations to document how Matchmaker advances, freezes, and re-anchors during alignment. An OSC interface transmits the per-frame stream to TouchDesigner, where the metrics drive a generative visualization. The resulting system illustrates the characteristic behaviors of Matchmaker’s alignment process in real time, including sensitivity to frame-rate settings, intermittent periods of clamping, and sudden re-anchoring events. Smoothing and simple hysteresis methods are applied within TouchDesigner to stabilize rapidly changing signals for visual use. The work provides a technical account of how the alignment metrics behave when surfaced directly to an external visualization environment.
